CodioOps solves this. Every GlobalCodio engagement includes a dedicated CodioOps team that does the configuration, optimization, and continuous tuning work that turns the platform into a productive operations engine for the firm.

**What CodioOps delivers:**
- Workflow configuration (built to the firm's exact case lifecycle)
- Template library (customized to the firm's case types, jurisdictions, and document standards)
- Form configuration and integration with CodioForms
- Process documentation (capturing the tribal knowledge that lives in staff heads)
- Automation setup (turning on the rules engines, reminders, escalations, and integrations)
- Best practice implementation (immigration-specific workflows informed by 1,500+ firms of experience)
- Continuous optimization (ongoing tuning as the firm evolves)
- Quarterly business reviews and platform usage audits
